Gloss — notices for the offline dictionaries in this folder
===========================================================

The JSON files beside this notice are reference data, not part of the Gloss
program. They were assembled by scripts/download-dictionaries.py in the Gloss
source repository from the sources below, and each remains under the terms of
its own source. Gloss's AGPL-3.0 licence covers the application only.

Source: https://github.com/shardhayv/pdf-reader-with-dictionary

       history, biology and medicine, chemistry, mathematics and physics,
       psychology and literature)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Definitions are taken from the "Glossary of …" and related list articles of
the English Wikipedia, combined with subject-filtered entries from Princeton

11  Devanagari — Hindi & Sanskrit
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
