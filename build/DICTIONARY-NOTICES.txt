Gloss — notices for the offline dictionaries in this folder
===========================================================

The JSON files beside this notice are reference data, not part of the Gloss
program. They are built by scripts/download-dictionaries.py --public in the
Gloss source repository. Each remains under the terms of its own source;
Gloss's AGPL-3.0 licence covers the application only.

Source: https://github.com/shardhayv/pdf-reader-with-dictionary

vocabulary newer than WordNet. The Gloss-written entries are in
